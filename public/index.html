Terms of Service

1. Purpose: This engine serves real-time peer communications over browser WebRTC standards.

2. Bandwidth: Voice packets stream direct between endpoints. The central node coordinates signaling data only.

3. Permitted Usage: Users are responsible for all transmissions initiated during room sessions.

Privacy Policy

1. Zero Storage: Audio streams bypass server storage and are transferred directly between peer machines.

2. Ephemeral Chat: Text messaging buffers are retained strictly in memory during an active session and deleted when empty.

3. Minimal Telemetry: No trackers, cookies, or profiling systems are bound to your transmission session.
